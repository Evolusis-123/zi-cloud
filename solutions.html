Challenges and outcomes

Challenges

  • Alert volumes that outpace analyst capacity, creating backlogs and prioritisation by intuition
  • Investigation notes spread across email, documents and case tools, making the decision trail hard to reconstruct
  • Quality assurance that samples after the fact, rather than controlling the process as it runs
  • Model and rule changes made without a documented approval route or back-testing evidence
  • Restricted SAR information leaking into general case channels, creating tipping-off risk

Outcomes

  • Shorter time from alert to disposition, with service levels that are measured rather than estimated
  • Consistent decision records that can be produced for a supervisory request within hours
  • Quality assurance built into the workflow, with defect rates visible to team leads
  • A controlled change process for rules, thresholds and routing logic
Common questions
Do you replace our screening or transaction monitoring system?

No. We design the workflows around the systems you already run and connect them with controlled integrations. Core monitoring engines remain systems of record, and our work sits in the operational layer where cases, evidence and decisions are managed.

Can AI be used in investigations?

Yes, within defined boundaries. Typical uses are drafting case summaries, extracting information from documents and surfacing relevant adverse media for review. The analyst remains accountable for the disposition, and every AI output is logged with its reviewer.

How do you handle SAR confidentiality?

SAR workflows are designed with restricted visibility, separate evidence stores where required, and gating controls that prevent the subject of a report being notified through routine case communications. Design choices are reviewed with your MLRO before build.

How long does a typical engagement take?

A focused workflow redesign for one process usually runs eight to twelve weeks. Broader programmes covering several processes are phased over several quarters, with each phase delivering working controls.

Challenges and outcomes

Challenges

  • Control libraries that drift from actual practice because nobody owns the link between them
  • Evidence held by individuals, making continuity dependent on who is available
  • Issues tracked in multiple trackers, with ageing and root cause invisible to committees
  • Policy attestation run as an annual email exercise with low completion assurance

Outcomes

  • A control inventory that second line can test and first line can operate without interpretation
  • Evidence requests answered from a single record, with reduced preparation time before reviews
  • Issue ageing and root cause visible to governance forums in a consistent format
  • Attestation completion tracked with escalation for overdue responses
Common questions
Do you provide compliance advice?

We design operations and controls to support your compliance framework. Regulatory interpretation and legal advice remain the responsibility of your compliance, legal and risk functions. We work to the obligations you define.

Can you work with our GRC platform?

Yes. Where a GRC platform is your system of record, we design the operational workflows around it and integrate evidence and issue data without duplicating the record. Where no platform exists, we build the operational layer on a controlled workflow platform.

How do you approach audit readiness?

We move evidence capture into the process so it is collected at the point of control operation. Readiness reviews then check completeness and quality against the examination scope, rather than attempting to reconstruct history.

Not a database. An operational control platform.

Audit and assurance

Where Airtable should not be used
  • As the system of record for transactions, balances or regulatory data that other systems must treat as authoritative
  • For high-volume, low-latency processing that belongs in an engine designed for throughput
  • Where access requirements cannot be met through permissions and the data must be partitioned at a deeper level
  • Where no owner will govern the base, its automations and its change history
