Common questions

Do you help us write an AI policy?

We can, but our focus is on the operating model that gives the policy effect: the use-case register, approval routes, tiering, monitoring and evidence. A policy that cannot be operated is a liability, so we design the mechanisms first and align the wording to them.

How does this relate to model risk management?

Where you have a model risk framework, we align tiering and validation expectations to it and extend it to cover generative and workflow-embedded AI, which often falls outside traditional model inventories.

Is human-in-the-loop always the right answer?

No. Human review adds cost and can become a rubber stamp if the reviewer lacks time or information. We set oversight by risk tier, and we measure whether reviewers are genuinely catching errors.

Can you assess AI tools that our teams already use?

Yes. Shadow use is common, and it is usually the first thing we surface in discovery. We help you bring existing tools into the register, classify them and decide which should be governed, restricted or retired.
