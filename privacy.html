Privacy Notice

How Zi Cloud Ltd collects, uses and protects personal information submitted through this website and in the course of enquiries.

Who we are

Zi Cloud Ltd is a specialist consultancy for regulated operations. This notice explains how we handle personal information you provide through this website, including enquiries, discovery call requests and partnership or vendor approaches.

What we collect

Name, work email address, organisation, role and the content of your message when you submit an enquiry. Technical information such as browser type and approximate location, collected through essential cookies that keep the site running.

We ask you not to include personal data about your customers or other individuals in enquiries. Where a process you describe contains personal data, we will discuss the appropriate handling before any further information is shared.

How we use it

We use enquiry information to respond to you, to arrange discovery calls, and to manage the relationship that follows. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use enquiry content to train third-party AI models.

Legal basis and retention

Where you contact us, we rely on taking steps at your request before entering a relationship. Enquiry records are kept for no longer than necessary to respond and to manage any resulting engagement, and are then deleted or anonymised.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and you may object to processing or withdraw consent for optional cookies at any time. Contact hello@zicloud.uk to exercise these rights.

Changes to this notice

We update this notice when our practices change. The date of the latest version is shown on the cookie policy page.
